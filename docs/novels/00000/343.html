SECTION 2: THE SCIENCE OF SYNERGY AND COMPATIBILITY

The efficacy of a skincare routine is determined not just by the ingredients themselves, but by how they are layered and how their chemical properties interact. PQQ, Ergothioneine, and Squalene form a highly compatible trio because they operate through distinct but complementary pathways. PQQ and Ergothioneine are primarily focused on cellular protection and energy, while Squalene focuses on the physical barrier.

From a chemical perspective, PQQ and Ergothioneine are generally stable in water-based formulations, making them ideal for serums. Because they do not require a specific acidic pH to function—unlike some exfoliating acids—they are less likely to cause irritation when combined. Squalene, being an oil-soluble lipid, serves as the perfect final step to seal in these water-based antioxidants. This layering strategy adheres to the fundamental principle of applying products from the thinnest, most water-soluble consistency to the thickest, most oil-rich consistency.

When these ingredients are used together, the Squalene acts as a buffer. It prevents the rapid evaporation of moisture, which in turn keeps the skin’s surface hydrated and receptive to the antioxidant benefits of PQQ and Ergothioneine. This synergy ensures that the skin remains hydrated throughout the day, which is a critical factor in preventing the formation of dehydration-induced fine lines. Community reviews consistently note that when these three ingredients are integrated into a single routine, the skin feels more balanced and less prone to the fluctuations in texture that often occur with seasonal changes.

SECTION 3: STEP-BY-STEP MORNING (AM) AND EVENING (PM) ROUTINE

To maximize the benefits of this trio, a structured approach to application is necessary. The goal is to ensure maximum absorption of the antioxidants while maintaining a robust moisture barrier.

For the morning routine, start with a gentle cleanser to remove impurities without stripping the skin of its natural oils. Once the skin is clean and slightly damp, apply a serum containing PQQ and Ergothioneine. These ingredients are ideal for daytime use because they provide a defensive shield against environmental stressors encountered throughout the day. Allow the serum to absorb for approximately sixty seconds. Follow this with a moisturizer that contains Squalene, or apply a few drops of pure Squalene oil to the skin. This lipid layer will lock in the antioxidants and provide a smooth base for sunscreen application. Sunscreen is non-negotiable in any age-defying routine, as it prevents the UV-induced breakdown of the very collagen that these ingredients are working to support.

In the evening, the routine should focus on replenishment and recovery. After cleansing, apply the PQQ and Ergothioneine serum again. Because the skin undergoes a natural repair process overnight, providing these antioxidants during this window can support the skin’s recovery efforts. If you are using other active ingredients, such as retinoids or peptides, apply them after the antioxidant serum but before the Squalene. Finally, finish with a generous application of a Squalene-based moisturizer. This will prevent transepidermal water loss while you sleep, ensuring that you wake up with skin that feels plump and well-nourished.

Reported experiences suggest that users who adhere to this consistent AM and PM protocol notice a significant improvement in the overall radiance of their complexion within four to six weeks. The key is to maintain this rhythm, as the cumulative effects of antioxidant protection and barrier support are most visible with long-term, daily use.

SECTION 4: SKIN TYPE ADAPTATIONS AND TOLERANCE BUILDING

One of the greatest advantages of PQQ, Ergothioneine, and Squalene is their universal compatibility. Unlike ingredients that require a gradual introduction to build tolerance, these three are generally well-tolerated by even the most sensitive skin profiles.

For those with dry skin, the focus should be on the concentration of Squalene. You may choose to layer a Squalene-rich serum under a heavier cream to ensure that the skin remains hydrated throughout the day. The lipid-replenishing properties of Squalene are particularly beneficial for mature skin, which often struggles to retain moisture.

For individuals with oily or blemish-prone skin, the lightweight nature of Squalene is a major benefit. Because it is non-comedogenic, it provides the necessary hydration without clogging pores or contributing to breakouts. When using PQQ and Ergothioneine, those with oily skin may prefer water-based serums that do not contain heavy waxes or butters, allowing the antioxidants to penetrate quickly without leaving a greasy residue.

For sensitive skin, the anti-stress properties of Ergothioneine are particularly valuable. It helps to calm the skin’s response to environmental triggers, making it an excellent choice for those who experience redness or discomfort when exposed to harsh weather or pollution. When introducing these ingredients, there is no need for a complex "patch testing" protocol, though it is always good practice to observe how your skin responds to any new product over the course of a few days.

PQQ and Ergothioneine are potent, but they must be properly formulated to remain active. When evaluating labels, look for the full ingredient name rather than just marketing buzzwords. High-quality serums will list PQQ or Ergothioneine within the first half of the ingredient list. For Squalene, ensure it is derived from sustainable sources, such as olives or sugarcane, which are common and effective alternatives to traditional sources.
