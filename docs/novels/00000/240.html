EVENING (PM) ROUTINE: CELLULAR RENEWAL AND BARRIER REPAIR

The evening routine is dedicated to supporting the skin's natural regeneration processes, focusing on collagen synthesis support and deep hydration.

STEP 1: DOUBLE CLEANSE

Begin with an oil-based cleanser to break down sunscreen, sebum, and environmental pollutants. Follow with your gentle water-based cleanser to ensure a perfectly clean canvas.

STEP 2: CALM & PREPARE (SYMSITIVE 1609)

Apply your SymSitive 1609 serum to clean skin. This prepares the skin barrier for the subsequent active ingredients, minimizing any potential sensitivity during the night.

STEP 3: RENEW (BAKUCHIOL)

Apply a lipid-rich Bakuchiol serum or emulsion. Gently massage the product into the skin using upward motions. Bakuchiol will work overnight to support collagen synthesis, promote cellular renewal, and target fine lines and wrinkles.

Community reviews consistently note that layering a water-based citric acid serum prior to a lipid-rich bakuchiol emulsion yields a visibly smoother skin texture within several weeks of consistent use, without the flaking typical of traditional retinoids.

STEP 4: NOURISH

Conclude the evening routine with an age-defying moisturizer rich in ceramides, fatty acids, and cholesterol. This locks in the active ingredients and reinforces the skin barrier while you sleep.

SKIN TYPE ADAPTATIONS & TOLERANCE BUILDING

While this trio is designed to be highly tolerable, individual skin types may require specific adjustments to maximize efficacy and comfort.

SENSITIVE SKIN PROFILES

For those with highly reactive or sensitive skin, introducing new active ingredients should always be a gradual process.

- Start by using the Citric Acid serum only twice a week in the morning.

- Always apply the SymSitive 1609 serum immediately afterward to mitigate any potential stinging.

- Introduce Bakuchiol in the evening on alternate nights, gradually building up to nightly use as your skin's tolerance increases.

- If any redness occurs, temporarily suspend the Citric Acid and focus solely on the SymSitive 1609 and a basic moisturizer to restore barrier equilibrium.

MATURE AND DRY SKIN PROFILES

Mature skin often suffers from a slowed rate of cellular turnover and a depleted lipid barrier. This skin type benefits immensely from the collagen-boosting properties of Bakuchiol and the exfoliating action of Citric Acid.

- To support dry skin, ensure that your Bakuchiol is formulated in a rich, nourishing oil or cream base rather than a lightweight gel.

- Consider incorporating a hydrating essence containing glycerin or panthenol between the Citric Acid and SymSitive 1609 steps to boost moisture levels.

- The combination of gentle exfoliation and deep lipid replenishment will help restore a youthful bounce and plumpness to mature skin.
