INGREDIENT BREAKDOWN AND BIOCHEMICAL ROLES

To understand the interaction between these components, we must first analyze their individual contributions to skin health. However, because of its potency, it can sometimes lead to temporary dryness or surface sensitivity, necessitating the use of supportive ingredients.

It is particularly valued for its ability to support the skin against the effects of glycation, a process that can impact the firmness and suppleness of the complexion over time.
