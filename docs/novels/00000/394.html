By maintaining optimal hydration levels within the stratum corneum, Ectoin supports the integrity of the skin barrier, ensuring that the complexion remains resilient and supple.

Glabridin, on the other hand, is a hydrophobic isoflavonoid derived from Glycyrrhiza glabra. Its primary role in a high-performance routine is the promotion of a uniform skin tone. When combined with the protective nature of Ectoin and the brightening capabilities of Glabridin, Matrixyl 3000 provides the necessary structural foundation for a comprehensive age-defying regimen.
