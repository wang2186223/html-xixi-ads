Chapter 522

Serena noticed the subtle tension flashing across his features, and part of her wanted to beg him to stay. Yet after a brief hesitation, she offered a supportive smile and replied with quiet understanding, "You should go. I'll be fine on my own."

Once Adrian had departed, Miranda quickly shut the door with a soft click and turned to Serena, her face alight with excitement. "You'll never guess who I ran into earlier!"

Absently tossing the reports into the trash, Serena arched a brow with mild interest. "Do tell."

"Vivian Hartley, along with her manager, Lydia Bennett," Miranda gushed, pulling a chair close to the bed, her voice thick with gossip. "They were deep in conversation. Turns out Vivian's recent leg injury, which she neglected, not only reopened today but got burned too. Rumor has it, it might even scar!"

Serena stopped fidgeting with her fingers, her attention sharpening. She recalled Dominic mentioning a fire at Vivian's family estate that fateful night in the dimly lit private room of Mirage Lounge. It was becoming clear Vivian had likely been present when the incident occurred.

So while Vivian was enduring a dangerous ordeal, Adrian had been tending to Serena, who merely had a minor ailment? A slow, triumphant smirk curled Serena's lips.

After Serena left, Adrian had rushed into marriage. At first, she thought it was to provoke her, but as time passed, she began questioning Vivian's true significance in Adrian's heart. Looking back, perhaps she'd overestimated Vivian's importance. Even with the wedding band on his finger, Serena knew she remained his priority.

With a barely concealed grin, she waved a dismissive hand. "Don't waste your energy on her."

To Serena, Vivian was already a defeated rival. Miranda, misinterpreting this as professional confidence, nodded eagerly. "Absolutely, with Mr. Blackwood supporting us, that role is as good as yours. We just need to wait for the official announcement."

"Speaking of which," Miranda casually shifted topics, "didn't you mention Vivian and Adrian were having marital problems? Think about it, Serena. Mr. Blackwood is an extraordinary catch. With so many women vying for him now that he might be single again, you should push harder for that divorce and secure your place as Mrs. Blackwood."

Serena remained silent at Miranda's strategic suggestion. While the logic was sound, she remembered how her previous impatience had backfired. Sometimes, patience yielded better results.

Meanwhile, Lydia felt a pang of concern leaving Vivian alone at the hotel. She swiftly arranged for Vivian to move into an apartment directly across from hers that very evening.

Vivian paused at her new doorstep, glancing curiously at the identical unit opposite hers. "Why do you own two apartments here when you live alone?" The location was prime real estate in the bustling city center, where one unit's price rivaled a suburban mansion.

Vivian marveled at Lydia's financial acumen, wondering just how substantial her wealth must be to make such purchases so casually.

Lydia responded with casual elegance, "I'd rather handpick my neighbors than risk ending up next to eccentric strangers. Purchasing the entire floor seemed the prudent choice. Fortunately, there are only two units per level."

Vivian realized Lydia wasn't merely wealthy - she came from old money.

After a thoughtful pause, Vivian murmured, "Thank you."

Lydia dismissed the gratitude with a wave. "Don't mention it. Once you start earning, we'll discuss rent," she said with quiet confidence.

The proposed rent was shockingly lower than Vivian's current budget hotel payments.

Their conversation halted when Lydia's phone buzzed. As she took the call, Vivian watched her agent's expression darken with each passing second.

Hanging up, Lydia scrolled through Twitter. The trending section exploded with #SerenaVivian hashtags, dominated by a bold headline: "EXCLUSIVE!" #SerenaHospitalizedFromOverwork #VivianTheDarkHorse.

The puzzle pieces clicked into place.

Paparazzi had captured Serena's latest hospital visit. While fans flooded social media with concern, others uncovered the production team had secretly scheduled callbacks, pushing Serena beyond her limits. She'd been juggling an impossible schedule, rehearsing until collapse.

The deeper netizens dug, the more explosive the revelations became. Serena, an industry darling with powerful backers, was unexpectedly competing against an unknown newcomer. Though Serena's performances were merely competent, she'd never lost a leading role since debut.

Until now.

Entertainment circles buzzed with one question: Who was this Vivian Hartley who could challenge Serena Whitmore?

Serena's fanbase erupted.

"This is absurd! If she were up against an A-lister, fine. But Vivian Hartley? Never heard of her! There's definitely shady business here!"

"Serena's connections alone should guarantee her roles. This nobody has zero credits to her name. Someone's pulling strings!"

"Pretty face, sure. But she's clearly sleeping her way to the top! Unlike our Serena who earned everything!"

"Wait... that name sounds familiar. Has she been lurking in the industry forever without making waves?"

A tsunami of backlash from Serena's devoted fans crashed down upon Vivian, fueling the scandal's wildfire. The heated debate drew swarms of spectators, their polarized opinions crackling like live wires across social media platforms.

"I was there during the first round of auditions supporting other hopefuls when I saw Vivian Hartley in person. She's absolutely luminous - a rare gem that outshines all these so-called A-list actresses. Let's be honest, Serena can't hold a candle to her!"

"Serena's acting range has plateaued since her debut. Every role since has been a carbon copy of the last - safe, predictable, and utterly uninspired. It's obvious she's coasting on industry connections rather than talent. No wonder she lost the role to someone fresh."

But Serena's loyalists swiftly mobilized to silence dissent. They circulated "proof" that Vivian - an unknown newcomer - couldn't possibly have beaten an established star like Serena without powerful backers pulling strings.

The entertainment world knew Serena's credentials were impeccable, her reputation built through years of careful cultivation. Yet here was Vivian, seemingly emerging from nowhere to challenge her - exactly the scenario Serena's fans painted of a pretty face propped up by some wealthy benefactor. Vivian's striking beauty only reinforced their narrative, making even neutral observers nod in reluctant agreement.

Human nature struggles to believe extraordinary beauty comes without hidden patronage. Overnight, Vivian's rising star imploded before she could even enjoy her breakthrough, her reputation in freefall.

In her sterile hospital suite, Serena scrolled through the social media storm with predatory satisfaction. A venomous smile curled her lips as she absorbed the vitriol directed at Vivian. The transformation was startling - where hours earlier she'd appeared wan and fragile during her confrontation with Adrian, now she radiated malicious vitality.

Meanwhile, Lydia's voice remained an island of calm in the tempest. "Don't waste energy worrying about the trending drama," she assured Vivian with unshakable confidence. "I'll handle the fallout. You just focus on resting at home."

After Vivian's grateful nod, Lydia hurried off. Alone in her spacious 90-square-meter loft, Vivian felt the weight of helplessness press down. The apartment, meticulously cleaned by Lydia, offered sanctuary with its minimalist white palette warmed by golden lighting and crisp new linens.
