STEP-BY-STEP MORNING (AM) AND EVENING (PM) ROUTINE

To maximize the benefits of these advanced ingredients, proper sequencing is vital. Applying products in the correct order based on pH, molecular weight, and vehicle type ensures maximum absorption and prevents product pilling. Below is a highly optimized, youth-preserving skincare protocol designed to integrate Sepiwhite, Squalane, and Azelaic Acid.

MORNING (AM) ROUTINE: CLARIFY, HYDRATE, AND PROTECT

The morning routine focuses on neutralizing environmental stressors, regulating sebum production, and maintaining a smooth, matte yet hydrated canvas for the day.

Step 1: Gentle Cleansing

Begin with a sulfate-free, non-stripping cleanser to remove overnight sebum without disrupting the delicate acid mantle.

Step 2: Hydrating Toner or Essence

Apply a lightweight, water-based essence rich in humectants like glycerin or hyaluronic acid. This dampens the skin, enhancing the penetration of subsequent active ingredients.

Step 3: Azelaic Acid and Squalane Emulsion

Apply a thin layer of your Azelaic Acid formula. If using a separate Squalane-based cream or a combined emulsion, apply it now. The Squalane will lock in the hydration from your essence while the Azelaic Acid works to keep pores clear and calm throughout the day.

Step 4: Age-Defying Moisturizer

Layer a lightweight, peptide-rich moisturizer to support collagen synthesis and provide firming support.

Step 5: Broad-Spectrum Sunscreen

Finish with a non-comedogenic, broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher. Sun protection is non-negotiable, as UV exposure triggers the very melanogenesis pathway that Sepiwhite and Azelaic Acid work so hard to inhibit.

EVENING (PM) ROUTINE: REPAIR, BRIGHTEN, AND RENEW

The evening routine is dedicated to cellular renewal, deep pigment correction, and barrier replenishment while the body is in its natural repair cycle.

Step 1: Double Cleansing

Start with a squalane-based cleansing oil or balm to dissolve sunscreen, sebum, and daily pollutants. Follow with your gentle water-based cleanser.

Step 2: Sepiwhite Serum

Apply a water-based or lightweight emulsion containing Sepiwhite. Because Sepiwhite targets the early stages of pigment production, applying it directly to clean skin allows the bio-vectorized amino acid to penetrate deeply and begin its receptor-blocking action.

Step 3: Collagen-Boosting Ingredients (Optional)

If your routine includes retinoids or peptides for wrinkle-smoothing and firming support, apply them here, allowing 3 to 5 minutes for absorption.

Step 4: Barrier-Replenishing Cream

Apply a lipid-rich moisturizer containing ceramides and fatty acids to support skin barrier resilience.
