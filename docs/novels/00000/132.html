A successful routine relies on the "thin-to-thick" principle, where products are applied based on their viscosity and absorption rate.

Morning (AM) Routine:

1. Cleansing: Start with a gentle, pH-balanced cleanser to remove impurities without stripping the skin of its natural oils.

2. Antioxidant Protection: Apply a serum containing Physalis Angulata extract. This provides the necessary defense against environmental stressors encountered throughout the day.

5. Sun Protection: Always finish with a broad-spectrum SPF to protect the skin from UV-induced damage, which is the primary cause of textural degradation.

Evening (PM) Routine:

1. Cleansing: Use a double-cleansing method if necessary to remove sunscreen and environmental pollutants.

2. Treatment: Apply the Copper Tripeptide-1 serum to clean, slightly damp skin. This is the optimal time for the skin to utilize the peptide’s structural support benefits.

3. Botanical Defense: Apply the Physalis Angulata extract to soothe the skin after a day of exposure.

For those with dry or mature skin, the routine can be enhanced by adding a lipid-rich moisturizer over the PGA-hydrogel in the evening. This "sandwiching" technique provides an extra layer of protection, ensuring that the skin remains nourished throughout the night. If the skin is particularly sensitive, it is recommended to introduce the Copper Tripeptide-1 serum slowly, perhaps starting with every other evening to allow the skin to acclimate to the active concentration.
