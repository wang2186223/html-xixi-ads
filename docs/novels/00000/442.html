SKIN TYPE ADAPTATIONS AND TOLERANCE BUILDING

Not all skin types will respond to this trio in the same way. For those with sensitive skin, the AHA complex should be introduced gradually. Start by using the AHA only two or three times per week, rather than daily. This allows the skin barrier to adjust to the increased rate of cellular turnover.

For oily or combination skin, the amount of Marula Oil used should be adjusted. While Marula oil is non-comedogenic, those prone to clogged pores may find that a single drop is sufficient to provide the necessary barrier support without feeling heavy. Focus the oil application on areas that tend to be drier, such as the cheeks or around the eyes, while using a lighter touch on the T-zone.

For mature skin, this routine is particularly beneficial. As the skin ages, the natural production of lipids decreases, and the rate of cellular turnover slows down. The combination of AHA-driven exfoliation and peptide-driven structural support helps to counteract these changes, promoting a more youthful, firm, and radiant appearance.

Reported experiences suggest that individuals with mature skin notice the most significant improvements in texture after four to six weeks of consistent use. The combination of these ingredients helps to smooth the appearance of fine lines and improves the overall luminosity of the complexion.

BUYER’S GUIDE AND LONG-TERM RADIANCE STRATEGY

When selecting products containing these ingredients, quality and formulation are paramount. Look for peptide serums that are housed in opaque or airless packaging, as peptides can be sensitive to light and air exposure. When choosing an AHA complex, look for formulations that include soothing agents, such as aloe or botanical extracts, to mitigate the potential for irritation.

Marula oil should ideally be cold-pressed and unrefined to ensure that the antioxidant profile remains intact. High-quality Marula oil will have a light, nutty scent and a pale yellow hue. Avoid products that contain synthetic fragrances or unnecessary fillers, as these can detract from the purity and efficacy of the active ingredients.
