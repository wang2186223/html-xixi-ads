STEP-BY-STEP MORNING (AM) & EVENING (PM) ROUTINE

To maximize the oil-regulating and age-defying benefits of these ingredients, they must be applied in a precise, thin-to-thick layering sequence that respects their chemical properties.

MORNING (AM) ROUTINE: MATTIFY, PROTECT, AND HYDRATE

The morning routine focuses on controlling daytime shine, providing antioxidant defense, and maintaining a smooth canvas for sunscreen and makeup application.

STEP 1: GENTLE CLEANSING

Begin with a sulfate-free, low-foaming gel cleanser. This removes overnight sebum accumulation without stripping the essential lipid barrier. Pat the skin dry with a clean towel, leaving it slightly damp.

STEP 2: THERMAL SPRING WATER MIST

Generously mist the face with Thermal Spring Water Extract. Allow it to sit on the skin for 30 seconds. Gently pat the excess with a tissue, leaving the skin hydrated and primed for active absorption.

STEP 3: CARNOSYL TRIPEPTIDE-30 SERUM

Apply 3 to 4 drops of a water-based Carnosyl Tripeptide-30 serum. Focus application on the T-zone (forehead, nose, and chin) where sebaceous glands are most active. Press the serum gently into the skin rather than rubbing, allowing the low-molecular-weight peptides to penetrate efficiently. Wait 1 to 2 minutes for complete absorption.

STEP 4: CHAMOMILE-INFUSED LIGHT EMULSION

Apply a lightweight, oil-free emulsion containing Chamomile Extract. This seals in the hydration from the thermal water and the active peptides, providing a soothing, protective barrier against environmental stressors.

STEP 5: BROAD-SPECTRUM SUNSCREEN

Conclude the morning routine with a fluid, non-comedogenic, broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher). Look for formulas containing silica or zinc oxide, which complement the oil-absorbing and soothing properties of your active ingredients.

EVENING (PM) ROUTINE: PURIFY, REPAIR, AND REGENERATE

The evening routine prioritizes deep skin recovery, cellular renewal, and intensive hydration to support the skin's natural nighttime repair cycle.

STEP 1: DOUBLE CLEANSE

Begin with a lightweight emulsifying cleansing oil or balm to dissolve sunscreen, sebum, and airborne pollutants. Follow with your gentle gel cleanser to ensure a pristine surface.

STEP 2: THERMAL SPRING WATER TONING

Saturate a soft cotton pad with Thermal Spring Water Extract and gently sweep it across the face to remove any residual minerals from tap water and restore the skin's optimal pH.

STEP 3: ACTIVE SYNERGY LAYER

In the evening, you can utilize a combined serum formulation containing both Carnosyl Tripeptide-30 and Chamomile Extract, or layer them individually. If layering individually, apply the Carnosyl Tripeptide-30 serum first to target the deeper epidermal layers, followed immediately by a Chamomile-rich serum to calm the skin surface.

STEP 4: AGE-DEFYING NIGHT CREAM

Apply a barrier-supportive night cream enriched with ceramides, niacinamide, and squalane. This formulation should lock in the active ingredients, preventing overnight moisture loss and supporting the skin's natural collagen synthesis support pathways.

Community reviews consistently note that adhering to this structured AM/PM sequence results in a visible refinement of pore texture and a more balanced, less reactive skin profile over a 28-day cellular turnover cycle.
