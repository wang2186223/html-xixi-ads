Unlike more aggressive brightening agents, Viniferine is often praised for its gentle nature, making it a staple for those who prioritize long-term skin clarity.

Glutathione, often referred to as a master antioxidant, plays a critical role in neutralizing environmental stressors that can lead to premature signs of aging. In topical applications, it works to support the skin’s natural defense mechanisms against oxidative stress, which is a primary driver of dullness and textural irregularities. By maintaining a healthy antioxidant environment, Glutathione helps preserve the skin’s youthful vitality and supports a smoother, more resilient appearance.

STEP-BY-STEP MORNING AND EVENING ROUTINE

Achieving the best results requires a disciplined approach to layering. The general rule of thumb in cosmetic chemistry is to apply products from the thinnest, most water-like consistency to the thickest, most lipid-rich texture.

In the evening, the focus shifts to repair and cellular renewal. After cleansing, you may choose to use a gentle exfoliant to remove dead skin cells, which prepares the skin to better absorb your active ingredients. Apply your Viniferine serum, followed by your Glutathione treatment. Because the skin undergoes natural repair processes while you sleep, this is the ideal time to use a more concentrated Panthenol-based night cream or sleeping mask. This provides the skin with the deep hydration and barrier support it needs to recover from the day.

User trials frequently highlight that following this thin-to-thick protocol significantly improves the absorption speed of the active ingredients. By allowing each layer to settle for approximately 60 seconds before applying the next, you ensure that the ingredients are not diluted and that they can function at their optimal capacity.
