The synergy between these ingredients is enhanced by the presence of Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride. Consumer feedback indicates that users often report a significant improvement in skin suppleness when using these ingredients in a triglyceride base. STEP-BY-STEP MORNING AND EVENING ROUTINE

To achieve the best results, the layering sequence must follow the principle of thin-to-thick application. If you are using a serum containing these ingredients, it should be applied after cleansing and toning, but before heavier creams or sun protection.

In the morning (AM), start with a gentle cleanser to remove impurities. Follow with a water-based hydrating toner or essence. Once the skin is slightly damp, apply your Physalys Angulata and Glabridin serum. The Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride base will help lock in the hydration provided by your toner. Allow the product to absorb for approximately 60 seconds before applying a broad-spectrum sunscreen. The antioxidant properties of the Physalys Angulata will complement your SPF, providing an extra layer of defense against environmental damage throughout the day.

In the evening (PM), the routine can be slightly more intensive. After double-cleansing, apply your active treatment. If you are using a retinol or a gentle exfoliant, apply those first, wait for them to fully absorb, and then layer your Physalys Angulata and Glabridin oil or emulsion over the top. This acts as a sealing layer, ensuring that the active ingredients remain in contact with the skin throughout the night while the Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride works to repair the moisture barrier.

User trials frequently highlight that the order of application is critical for comfort. When these ingredients are layered correctly, they provide a smooth, velvet-like finish that prepares the skin for the next step in the routine. Community reviews consistently note that the inclusion of a triglyceride-based product helps to mitigate any potential dryness associated with other potent actives in a routine, making the overall experience more pleasant and sustainable.
