STEP-BY-STEP MORNING AND EVENING ROUTINE

A successful routine is built on the principle of consistency and proper sequencing. In the morning, the goal is to provide antioxidant defense and hydration. Start with a gentle cleanser containing Capryloyl Glycine to remove impurities while respecting the skin barrier. Follow this with a Syn-Hycan serum, applying a few drops to the face and neck. Allow the serum to absorb for approximately sixty seconds. Next, apply a Bakuchiol-based serum or moisturizer. Because Bakuchiol is photostable, it is safe for daytime use and provides a protective layer that complements your broad-spectrum sunscreen.

The evening routine focuses on repair and cellular renewal. Begin with a thorough cleanse to remove environmental pollutants. If your skin is prone to congestion, a toner with Capryloyl Glycine is highly beneficial to refine the skin texture and prepare it for the actives. Apply your Syn-Hycan treatment first, as its peptide structure benefits from direct contact with the skin. Follow this with your Bakuchiol treatment. Wait times between layers are often debated, but the most effective approach is to wait until the skin feels dry to the touch before applying the next product. Individuals with dry or mature skin may find that they need additional lipid support.
