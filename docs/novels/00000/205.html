STEP-BY-STEP MORNING AND EVENING ROUTINE

Integrating these ingredients into a daily regimen requires a strategic approach based on molecular weight and product texture. For the best results, follow the thin-to-thick rule, ensuring that water-based serums are applied before lipid-rich moisturizers.

In the morning (AM), the focus should be on protection and hydration. Start with a gentle cleanser to remove any impurities from the night. If you are using a serum containing Capryloyl Glycine, apply this to clean, dry skin. Allow it to absorb for 60 seconds. Follow with a hydrating serum or a lightweight moisturizer. Because Tartaric Acid can increase skin sensitivity to environmental factors, it is mandatory to finish your morning routine with a broad-spectrum sunscreen. This protects the newly refined skin surface and supports the long-term anti-aging benefits of your routine.

In the evening (PM), you can leverage the resurfacing benefits of Tartaric Acid. After cleansing, apply your Tartaric Acid-based treatment. If your product also contains SymSitive 1609, you will likely find that you can apply it directly to the skin without the need for a buffer. If you are layering separate products, apply the acid first, wait for it to fully absorb, and then follow with a barrier-repairing moisturizer that contains ceramides or fatty acids. This layering sequence ensures that the active ingredients have direct contact with the skin, while the final moisturizer locks in hydration and supports the skin’s overnight recovery process.

Use the acid treatment two to three times a week, and always follow with a rich, lipid-replenishing cream. The goal is to maintain the skin’s moisture barrier while still benefiting from the cellular renewal properties of the acid.

Sensitive skin types should start with a "patch test" approach. Apply the product to a small area of the neck or behind the ear for several days before applying it to the entire face. Because SymSitive 1609 is designed to mitigate sensitivity, it is highly likely that even those with reactive skin will find this combination tolerable. However, consistency is more important than frequency. It is better to use the products consistently at a lower frequency than to over-apply and risk compromising the skin barrier.
