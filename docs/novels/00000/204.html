STEP-BY-STEP MORNING AND EVENING ROUTINE

A successful routine follows the principle of applying products from the thinnest to the thickest consistency. This ensures maximum absorption and prevents the formation of a barrier that might block subsequent ingredients.

In the morning, begin with a gentle cleanser to remove impurities without stripping the skin of its natural oils. While the skin is still slightly damp, apply a Hyaluronic Acid serum. This is the most critical step for hydration, as the humectant will pull the residual water into the skin. Allow this to absorb for approximately sixty seconds. Next, apply a moisturizer containing Pro-Xylane. This will provide the structural support needed for the day ahead. If the skin feels particularly dry, a single drop of Rosehip Seed Oil can be mixed into the moisturizer before application. Always conclude the morning routine with a broad-spectrum sunscreen, as UV exposure is the primary cause of premature textural changes.

In the evening, the focus shifts toward repair and nourishment. After cleansing, apply the Hyaluronic Acid serum again to replenish moisture lost during the day. Follow this with the Pro-Xylane treatment to support cellular renewal throughout the night. Finally, apply a more generous amount of Rosehip Seed Oil as the final step. This acts as an occlusive layer, locking in all the previous products and ensuring the skin remains hydrated until morning. User trials frequently highlight that this evening protocol is instrumental in waking up to a complexion that appears refreshed and smoothed.

SKIN TYPE ADAPTATIONS AND TOLERANCE BUILDING

Adapting this routine to specific skin types is straightforward due to the gentle nature of these ingredients. For those with oily or blemish-prone skin, the focus should be on the Hyaluronic Acid and Pro-Xylane components. Rosehip Seed Oil can still be used, but in smaller quantities, perhaps only a few times per week or applied only to areas that feel dry, such as the cheeks. Because Rosehip Seed Oil is non-comedogenic, it is generally well-tolerated even by those who are prone to breakouts.

For individuals with dry or mature skin, the routine can be intensified. Hyaluronic Acid can be layered in multiple applications, or a thicker cream containing Pro-Xylane can be used. Rosehip Seed Oil is particularly beneficial for this group and can be used liberally as a final step to provide a protective lipid barrier. Those with sensitive skin will appreciate the lack of harsh acids or fragrances often found in these formulations, making this a reliable routine for maintaining barrier health without the risk of redness or irritation.

When introducing these ingredients, it is always wise to observe how the skin responds. While these components are generally mild, consistency is the key to observing long-term benefits. If the skin feels overly saturated, reduce the frequency of the oil application. If the skin feels tight, increase the hydration step by ensuring the skin is damp before applying the Hyaluronic Acid.
