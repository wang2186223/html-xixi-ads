EVENING (PM) ROUTINE: CELLULAR RENEWAL AND BARRIER REPAIR

The evening routine focuses on supporting the skin's natural overnight repair cycle, promoting cellular renewal, and delivering deep nourishment.

STEP 1: DOUBLE CLEANSING

Use a lipid-soluble cleansing balm or oil to dissolve sunscreen, makeup, and airborne pollutants, followed by a gentle water-based cleanser to ensure a perfectly clean canvas.

STEP 2: GENTLE EXFOLIATION (2-3 TIMES A WEEK)

Incorporate a mild enzymatic or polyhydroxy acid (PHA) exfoliant to remove dead skin cells and promote textural refinement. This step enhances the absorption of the active ingredients that follow.

STEP 3: HIGH-CONCENTRATION PEPTIDE SERUM

Apply a concentrated serum featuring Palmitoyl Tetrapeptide-7. Applying the peptide directly to freshly cleansed, slightly damp skin allows for maximum penetration and structural signaling during the skin's peak regeneration hours.

STEP 4: LIPID-RICH COENZYME Q10 AND ASTAXANTHIN NIGHT CREAM

Apply a richer, lipid-dense night cream or facial oil containing Coenzyme Q10 and Astaxanthin. The lipid-rich base mimics the skin's natural sebum, providing deep barrier support and preventing overnight moisture loss.

Consumer feedback indicates that a thin-to-thick layering sequence in the evening, ending with a lipid-rich astaxanthin cream, results in a highly nourished, radiant complexion upon waking, without leaving a greasy residue.

SKIN TYPE ADAPTATIONS & TOLERANCE BUILDING

While this powerhouse combination is exceptionally well-tolerated by most skin types, tailoring the vehicle (the product texture) to your specific skin profile will yield the best results.

DRY AND MATURE SKIN

Dry and mature skin profiles benefit most from lipid-rich formulations. Look for creams and facial oils that combine Astaxanthin and Coenzyme Q10 with nourishing emollients such as squalane, ceramides, and evening primrose oil. These ingredients work synergistically to repair a compromised skin barrier, lock in deep hydration, and restore a youthful plumpness to depleted skin.

OILY AND COMBINATION SKIN

For oily or combination skin, lightweight, water-based gel-serums are the ideal delivery system. Look for formulations where Astaxanthin and Coenzyme Q10 are encapsulated in liposomes or nano-emulsions, which allow for rapid absorption without adding heavy lipids to the skin surface. Combining these actives with niacinamide can also help regulate sebum production while supporting pore refinement.

SENSITIVE SKIN

Unlike some high-potency active ingredients that can cause redness, flaking, or barrier disruption, Astaxanthin, Palmitoyl Tetrapeptide-7, and Coenzyme Q10 are inherently soothing. They help to calm the skin and support barrier resilience. However, sensitive skin types should always perform a patch test on the inner forearm before introducing new formulations to their daily routine.
