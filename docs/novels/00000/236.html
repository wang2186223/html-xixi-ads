EVENING (PM) ROUTINE: REPAIR AND REGENERATE

The evening routine focuses on cellular renewal, deep nourishment, and supporting the skin's natural nighttime repair cycle.

STEP 1: DOUBLE CLEANSE

Start with an oil-based cleanser to break down sunscreen, makeup, and pollutants, followed by a gentle water-based cleanser to ensure a clean canvas.

STEP 2: RESURFACING OR HYDRATING TONER

If desired, use a gentle exfoliating toner (such as one containing PHA or a mild lactic acid) twice a week to assist with surface desquamation. On other nights, stick to your hydrating Xylitylglucoside toner.

STEP 3: THE POWER SERUM

Apply a comprehensive serum containing both Tripeptide-1 and Xylitylglucoside. This combination supports nighttime dermal remodeling and prevents trans-epidermal water loss (TEWL) while you sleep.

STEP 4: NOURISHING NIGHT CREAM

Apply a richer cream infused with Asiatic Acid. The lipid-rich nature of a night cream helps Asiatic Acid work overnight to soothe the skin, reduce redness, and rebuild a compromised barrier.

User trials frequently highlight that adhering to this structured AM and PM sequence results in a significant improvement in skin texture, with many noting that their skin feels exceptionally soft and looks visibly more radiant upon waking.

SKIN TYPE ADAPTATIONS & TOLERANCE BUILDING

While this trio of ingredients is inherently gentle and well-tolerated by most skin types, customizing the application method can optimize results based on your specific skin profile.

DRY AND MATURE SKIN

Dry and mature skin types often suffer from a slow rate of natural desquamation and a depleted lipid barrier, making dark spots appear more pronounced. For these profiles, focus heavily on the hydrating benefits of Xylitylglucoside and the structural support of Tripeptide-1. Look for rich, lipid-based creams containing Asiatic Acid to lock in moisture and prevent dryness-induced dullness.

OILY AND BLEMISH-PRONE SKIN

Oily skin types should opt for lightweight, water-based gel-serums containing Tripeptide-1 and Xylitylglucoside. Asiatic Acid is highly beneficial for blemish-prone skin, as its soothing properties help calm the redness associated with active breakouts, thereby preventing the formation of post-blemish dark marks. Choose oil-free, non-comedogenic formulations to avoid clogging pores.

SENSITIVE SKIN

Unlike aggressive brightening agents like high-percentage retinoids or strong exfoliating acids, Asiatic Acid, Tripeptide-1, and Xylitylglucoside are highly supportive of sensitive skin. Asiatic Acid actively works to reduce skin sensitivity by reinforcing the stratum corneum. However, it is always wise to introduce any new active ingredients gradually, starting three times a week and building up to twice-daily use as tolerance is established.
