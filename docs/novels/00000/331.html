SECTION 4: SKIN TYPE ADAPTATIONS AND TOLERANCE BUILDING

While the combination of Dipeptide-2, Beta-Arbutin, and Squalene is generally suitable for all skin types, minor adjustments can be made to optimize results. Community reviews consistently note that even those with oily skin find Squalene to be surprisingly lightweight and non-greasy, provided the correct formulation is chosen.

For those with dry or mature skin, the routine can be bolstered by layering a thicker, more occlusive cream over the Squalene oil in the evening. Tolerance building is essential when introducing any new ingredient. Even though these three ingredients are known for their gentle profiles, it is always wise to perform a patch test on the inner forearm before applying them to the entire face. Start by using them once a day, preferably in the evening, before transitioning to twice-daily use as the skin becomes accustomed to the new regimen.

Bidens Pilosa extract is a botanical ingredient often categorized as a retinoid-mimetic.
