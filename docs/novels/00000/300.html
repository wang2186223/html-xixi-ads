STEP-BY-STEP MORNING (AM) & EVENING (PM) ROUTINE

To maximize the benefits of these advanced ingredients, it is crucial to understand how to layer active ingredients correctly. Below is a highly optimized, thin-to-thick sequencing protocol designed to preserve skin barrier resilience while delivering maximum age-defying support.

MORNING (AM) ROUTINE: PROTECTION AND RADIANCE

The primary goal of the morning routine is to shield the skin from environmental aggressors, such as ultraviolet radiation and pollution, while boosting surface radiance.

STEP 1: GENTLE CLEANSE

Begin with a mild, non-foaming cleanser to remove overnight sebum without stripping the skin's natural lipids. Pat dry gently with a clean towel.

STEP 2: RADIANCE BOOSTING SERUM (MAP)

Apply a generous layer of a water-based serum containing Magnesium Ascorbyl Phosphate (MAP). This provides immediate antioxidant defense against daytime free radicals and supports collagen synthesis throughout the day.

STEP 3: CELLULAR ENERGIZING EMULSION (PQQ)

Once the MAP serum has fully absorbed (approximately 1 to 2 minutes), apply your PQQ-infused serum or light emulsion. This step infuses the skin with cellular energy, helping to maintain a plump, firm appearance.

STEP 4: AGE-DEFYING MOISTURIZER

Apply a lightweight, lipid-rich moisturizer containing ceramides and fatty acids to lock in hydration and reinforce the skin barrier.

STEP 5: BROAD-SPECTRUM SUNSCREEN

Finish with a mineral or chemical sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher. Antioxidants like MAP and PQQ work synergistically with sunscreen to provide enhanced protection against photoaging and wrinkle formation.

EVENING (PM) ROUTINE: RENEWAL AND REPAIR

The evening routine focuses on cellular renewal, gentle exfoliation, and deep hydration while the body is in its natural repair cycle.

STEP 1: DOUBLE CLEANSE

Use a gentle cleansing oil or balm to dissolve sunscreen and makeup, followed by a water-based cleanser to ensure a perfectly clean canvas.

STEP 2: TEXTURAL REFINEMENT (MICRO-ENCAPSULATED SALICYLIC ACID)

Apply a thin layer of a micro-encapsulated salicylic acid serum. Focus on areas prone to textural roughness, clogged pores, or fine lines. Because of the slow-release technology, there is no need to rinse this product off.

STEP 3: HYDRATION BUFFER (OPTIONAL)

If your skin is naturally dry, wait 3 to 5 minutes after applying the salicylic acid, then apply a simple hyaluronic acid serum to boost moisture levels.

STEP 4: NOURISHING NIGHT CREAM

Apply a rich, age-defying moisturizer. Look for formulas that contain peptides and squalane to support the skin's natural nighttime recovery process.

Reported experiences suggest that using MAP in the morning and micro-encapsulated salicylic acid in the evening optimizes skin clarity and brightness within four to six weeks of consistent use.

SKIN TYPE ADAPTATIONS & TOLERANCE BUILDING

While this trio is exceptionally gentle compared to traditional retinoids or high-strength alpha-hydroxy acids (AHAs), tailoring the application to your specific skin profile will yield the best results.
