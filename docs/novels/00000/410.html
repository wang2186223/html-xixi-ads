By encouraging the appearance of a more structured and resilient surface, these ingredients are highly sought after for those targeting the visible signs of time.

Acetyl Tetrapeptide-11 is a specialized peptide focused on structural support. It is often categorized as a signal peptide, working to reinforce the connection between the epidermis and the dermis. By supporting the density and integrity of the skin, this peptide contributes to a smoother, more refined surface texture. When used consistently, it helps maintain the appearance of elasticity, making it a cornerstone for those focused on age-defying results.

Niacinamide, or Vitamin B3, serves as the ultimate balancing agent in this trio. Its role is multifaceted: it supports the skin’s natural barrier function, assists in the regulation of sebum, and promotes an even-toned complexion. Unlike the highly specific signaling action of the peptides, Niacinamide provides broad-spectrum support, improving the skin’s ability to retain moisture and defend against environmental stressors. Its high compatibility with other actives makes it the ideal partner for complex peptide-based routines.

STEP-BY-STEP MORNING AND EVENING ROUTINE

Creating a routine that maximizes the benefits of these ingredients requires a disciplined approach to layering. The golden rule of skincare layering is to apply products from the thinnest consistency to the thickest.
