SECTION 4: SKIN TYPE ADAPTATIONS AND TOLERANCE BUILDING

While the combination of Beta-Glucan, Decarboxy Carnosine HCL, and Nonapeptide-1 is generally suitable for all skin types, minor adjustments can be made to tailor the routine to specific needs.

For OILY SKIN: Focus on water-based serums. Avoid heavy creams and opt for gel-based formulas that feature Beta-Glucan as the primary humectant. The goal is to provide hydration without adding excess lipids that could clog pores.

For SENSITIVE SKIN: Introduce the ingredients one at a time. Start with the Beta-Glucan serum for one week to ensure your skin barrier is comfortable before adding the peptide-rich serum. For MATURE SKIN: Focus on the layering of the peptides. You may choose to use a higher concentration of Decarboxy Carnosine HCL in the evening to maximize antioxidant defense.
