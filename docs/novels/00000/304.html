The molecular weights of these three actives are ideally suited for topical delivery:

- Ergothioneine: ~229 g/mol

- Phloretin: ~274 g/mol

- Thiamidol: ~281 g/mol

According to the "500 Dalton Rule," molecules under 500 g/mol can readily penetrate the skin barrier. Because all three ingredients fall well below this threshold, they do not require invasive delivery systems to reach the deeper epidermal and dermal layers. Phloretin’s ability to act as a natural penetration enhancer further accelerates the absorption of Ergothioneine and Thiamidol, creating a highly efficient delivery cascade that supports collagen synthesis without disrupting the delicate lipid barrier.

Reported experiences suggest that users transitioning to a regimen combining these three actives notice a significant improvement in skin bounce and overall luminosity within four to six weeks, without the redness or flaking often associated with traditional retinoids.

THE STEP-BY-STEP LAYERING PROTOCOL: MORNING AND EVENING ROUTINES

To safely integrate Thiamidol, Ergothioneine, and Phloretin into your daily regimen, follow this scientifically optimized, thin-to-thick layering sequence. This protocol is designed to maximize antioxidant defense during the day and support cellular renewal and collagen preservation overnight.

MORNING (AM) ROUTINE: THE ANTIOXIDANT SHIELD

The primary goal of the morning routine is to protect the skin from UV-induced oxidative stress, infrared radiation, and environmental pollution—the primary drivers of collagen degradation.

STEP 1: CLEANSE

Begin with a gentle, non-stripping, pH-balanced cleanser to remove overnight sebum without disrupting the skin barrier.

STEP 2: PREPARE AND PENETRATE (PHLORETIN)

Apply a lightweight, water-based Phloretin serum to clean, dry skin. Phloretin’s acidic nature prepares the skin and enhances the absorption of subsequent steps. Allow this to absorb for 2 to 3 minutes.

STEP 3: ENERGIZE AND HYDRATE (ERGOTHIONEINE)

Apply a hydrating serum containing Ergothioneine, preferably paired with low-molecular-weight hyaluronic acid or glycerin. This step infuses the cells with mitochondrial protection and deep hydration, plumping the skin and smoothing fine lines.

STEP 4: LOCK IN HYDRATION

Apply a lightweight, age-defying moisturizer containing ceramides and fatty acids to seal in the active ingredients and support skin barrier resilience.

STEP 5: BROAD-SPECTRUM PROTECTION

Finish with a high-protection, broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher). Sunscreen is the ultimate collagen-preserving step, as it prevents UV rays from triggering the enzymatic breakdown of the dermal matrix.

EVENING (PM) ROUTINE: CELLULAR RENEWAL AND REPAIR

The evening routine focuses on repairing daytime damage, evening out skin tone, and supporting the natural nocturnal synthesis of structural proteins.

STEP 1: DOUBLE CLEANSE

Use a lipid-soluble cleansing oil or balm followed by a water-based cleanser to thoroughly remove sunscreen, pollutants, and makeup.

STEP 2: CORRECT AND EVEN (THIAMIDOL)

Apply a targeted Thiamidol serum or emulsion. Nighttime is the optimal period for pigment correction, as the skin's natural cellular turnover rate peaks during sleep.

STEP 3: RECOVER AND REBUILD (ERGOTHIONEINE)

Layer an Ergothioneine-rich cream or fluid. This supports mitochondrial recovery and cellular repair processes while the body is at rest, ensuring optimal conditions for collagen synthesis support.
