STEP-BY-STEP MORNING (AM) AND EVENING (PM) ROUTINE

To maximize the benefits of these advanced ingredients, it is essential to follow a structured, thin-to-thick layering protocol. This ensures optimal absorption and prevents product pilling. Below is a highly effective, youth-preserving skincare routine designed specifically for sensitive skin.

MORNING (AM) ROUTINE: DEFENSE AND RADIANCE

Step 1: Gentle Cleansing

Begin with a sulfate-free, non-foaming cleanser to remove overnight impurities without stripping the skin's natural lipids. Pat dry gently with a clean microfiber towel.

Step 2: Brightening and Sebum Control

Apply a few drops of a serum featuring Potassium Azeloyl Diglycinate. Press the product gently into the skin, focusing on areas prone to redness, uneven tone, or excess oil production. Allow it to absorb for 60 seconds.

Step 3: Intense Hydration

Apply a lightweight gel-serum containing PGA-Hydrogel. This will lock in the active ingredients from the previous step and provide a continuous reservoir of moisture throughout the day, creating a smooth, plump canvas for makeup or sunscreen.

Step 4: Barrier Support and Protection

Follow with a lightweight, fragrance-free moisturizer containing ceramides to further reinforce the lipid barrier. Finish with a broad-spectrum mineral sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher) to protect the skin from UV-induced aging and sensitivity.

EVENING (PM) ROUTINE: REPAIR AND REJUVENATION

Step 1: Double Cleansing

Start with a gentle, emulsifying oil or balm cleanser to break down sunscreen, sebum, and environmental pollutants. Follow with your gentle AM water-based cleanser to ensure a perfectly clean, receptive base.

Step 2: Structural Remodeling

Apply a dedicated peptide serum containing Hexapeptide-9. Focus on areas prone to expression lines, such as the forehead, around the eyes, and the nasolabial folds. This allows the collagen-boosting ingredients to work undisturbed during the skin's nocturnal repair cycle.

Step 3: Deep Moisture Infusion

Layer your PGA-Hydrogel serum over the peptide serum. This creates an occlusive, hydrating micro-mesh that prevents trans-epidermal water loss during sleep, which is when the skin barrier is most vulnerable to dehydration.

Step 4: Nourishing Night Cream

Conclude the routine with a rich, age-defying moisturizer containing essential fatty acids and phytosterols. This helps seal in the active serums and supports the skin's natural lipid barrier recovery overnight.

SKIN TYPE ADAPTATIONS AND TOLERANCE BUILDING

While this triad of ingredients is inherently gentle, tailoring the application to your specific skin profile can further optimize results and ensure maximum comfort.

For Dry and Mature Skin:

Dry skin types often require additional lipid support. To enhance this routine, look for formulations that combine PGA-Hydrogel with nourishing plant oils (such as squalane or jojoba oil) or cholesterol. This ensures that the skin receives both water-based hydration and lipid-based nourishment, which is essential for maintaining a youthful bounce and preventing crepey texture.

For Oily and Blemish-Prone Skin:

Those with oily, sensitive skin will benefit greatly from the sebum-regulating properties of Potassium Azeloyl Diglycinate. In this case, lightweight, water-based gel formulations are preferred. A simple routine consisting of a PAD serum followed by a pure PGA-Hydrogel moisturizer can provide all the hydration and brightening benefits required without adding heavy, pore-clogging lipids.

For Highly Reactive or Compromised Skin:

If your skin barrier is severely compromised, it is wise to introduce new active ingredients gradually. Start by incorporating the PGA-Hydrogel for one week to build up hydration levels. Once the skin feels comfortable, introduce the Hexapeptide-9 serum in the evening, followed by the Potassium Azeloyl Diglycinate serum in the morning. This phased approach allows the skin to adapt without overwhelming its sensory pathways.
