STEP 1: DOUBLE CLEANSE

Start with an oil-based cleanser to dissolve makeup and sunscreen, followed by a water-based cleanser to thoroughly clean the skin.

STEP 2: KAOLIN CLAY MASK (2-3 TIMES PER WEEK)

Apply a thin, even layer of a Kaolin Clay mask to clean, dry skin. Leave it on for 8 to 10 minutes. It is crucial to rinse the mask off before it completely dries and cracks, as this prevents the clay from drawing moisture out of the skin. This step purifies the pores and refines skin texture.

STEP 3: HYDRATING TONER OR ESSENCE

After rinsing off the clay mask, apply a hydrating toner or essence rich in humectants, such as glycerin or hyaluronic acid, to replenish surface hydration.

STEP 4: THD ASCORBATE NIGHT CREAM OR FACIAL OIL

Finish your evening routine with a rich cream or facial oil containing THD Ascorbate. The lipid-soluble nature of THD Ascorbate makes it ideal for overnight use, as it works with the skin's natural lipid production to deeply hydrate, repair, and support collagen synthesis while you sleep.

User trials frequently highlight that applying a lipid-soluble THD Ascorbate cream immediately after a gentle Kaolin Clay mask session helps to instantly replenish lost moisture, leaving the skin feeling exceptionally plump and soft rather than tight.

SKIN TYPE ADAPTATIONS & TOLERANCE BUILDING

While O-Ethyl Ascorbic Acid, Kaolin Clay, and THD Ascorbate are suitable for most skin types, customizing their application ensures optimal results and prevents barrier fatigue.

DRY AND DEHYDRATED SKIN

For dry skin, the primary goal is to maximize lipid-rich hydration and prevent trans-epidermal water loss (TEWL).

- Focus heavily on THD Ascorbate, which can be used daily in the form of a rich, nourishing face oil or cream.

- Limit the use of Kaolin Clay to once a week, and look for clay masks that are blended with hydrating botanical oils or honey to prevent any drying sensation.

- Layer O-Ethyl Ascorbic Acid under a hyaluronic acid serum to boost surface hydration.

OILY AND BLEMISH-PRONE SKIN

Oily skin benefits greatly from the sebum-regulating properties of Kaolin Clay and the lightweight nature of O-Ethyl Ascorbic Acid.

- Use the Kaolin Clay mask 2 to 3 times a week to keep pores clear of excess sebum and reduce the occurrence of breakouts and clogged pores.

- Opt for a lightweight, water-based O-Ethyl Ascorbic Acid serum for daily use, as it provides antioxidant protection without adding excess weight to the skin.

- Choose a fluid, non-comedogenic lotion containing THD Ascorbate to ensure deep hydration without clogging pores.

SENSITIVE SKIN

Sensitive skin types often struggle with traditional vitamin C formulations, making this stable, non-acidic combination an ideal alternative.

- Introduce O-Ethyl Ascorbic Acid and THD Ascorbate gradually, starting 2 to 3 times a week, and monitor for any signs of redness.

- Ensure the Kaolin Clay mask is free of added fragrances or essential oils, which can trigger sensitivity.

- Always follow the clay mask immediately with a barrier-supporting moisturizer to maintain skin comfort.

Community reviews consistently note that individuals with sensitive skin profiles experience far fewer instances of irritation when transitioning from traditional L-ascorbic acid to a dual-derivative routine featuring O-Ethyl Ascorbic Acid and THD Ascorbate.
