The focus of the morning routine is antioxidant defense, deep hydration, and protection against environmental stressors that trigger melanin production.

STEP 1: GENTLE CLEANSE

Begin with a mild, non-stripping, water-based cleanser to remove overnight sebum and impurities without disrupting the delicate lipid barrier.

STEP 2: HYDRATING AND PROTECTING SERUM (HYDROLYZED HYALURONIC ACID & DECARBOXY CARNOSINE HCL)

Apply a water-based serum containing Hydrolyzed Hyaluronic Acid and Decarboxy Carnosine HCl to damp skin. The low molecular weight hyaluronic acid will draw moisture deep into the epidermis, while the Decarboxy Carnosine HCl provides immediate antioxidant protection against daytime environmental aggressors. Allow 1 to 2 minutes for full absorption.

STEP 3: LIGHTWEIGHT MOISTURIZER

Apply a lightweight, barrier-supportive lotion or cream containing ceramides or niacinamide to lock in the active serum and provide a smooth canvas.

STEP 4: BROAD-SPECTRUM SUNSCREEN (CRITICAL STEP)

Apply a generous amount of broad-spectrum sunscreen with an SPF of 30 or higher. Sunscreen is non-negotiable in any dark spot correcting routine, as UV radiation is the primary catalyst for melanocyte activation. The antioxidants in your serum will work synergistically with your sunscreen to provide superior environmental defense.

EVENING (PM) ROUTINE: REPAIR AND NOURISHMENT

The focus of the evening routine is deep barrier repair, cellular renewal, and intense lipid replenishment.

STEP 1: DOUBLE CLEANSE

Begin with a gentle oil-based cleanser to dissolve sunscreen, sebum, and airborne pollutants accumulated throughout the day. Follow with a gentle water-based cleanser to ensure a perfectly clean, receptive canvas.

STEP 2: ACTIVE SERUM (HYDROLYZED HYALURONIC ACID & DECARBOXY CARNOSINE HCL)

Reapply your water-based serum containing Hydrolyzed Hyaluronic Acid and Decarboxy Carnosine HCl. During sleep, the skin's natural repair mechanisms are highly active, making this the optimal time for Decarboxy Carnosine HCl to support collagen synthesis and combat glycation.

STEP 3: BARRIER-SUPPORTIVE NIGHT CREAM

Apply a nourishing night cream to provide essential structural lipids and support overnight cellular renewal.

STEP 4: LIPID SEAL (MARULA OIL)

As the final step in your evening routine, warm 2 to 3 drops of pure, cold-pressed Marula Oil between your palms and gently press it onto your face and neck. This lipid-rich layer acts as an occlusive shield, locking in the hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid and active peptides, preventing overnight moisture loss, and deeply nourishing the skin barrier while you sleep.

SKIN TYPE ADAPTATIONS AND TOLERANCE BUILDING

While this ingredient combination is exceptionally gentle and biocompatible, tailoring the application to your specific skin type will yield the best possible results.

DRY AND MATURE SKIN TYPES

Dry and mature skin profiles will benefit immensely from the rich, emollient properties of Marula Oil. For these skin types, Marula Oil can be used both morning and evening. In the morning, mix a single drop of Marula Oil into your daily moisturizer for an added boost of radiance and barrier protection. In the evening, increase the amount to 3 to 4 drops as a final sealing step. The deep-penetrating hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid will address structural dehydration, while the anti-glycation properties of Decarboxy Carnosine HCl will help restore elasticity and firmness to mature skin.

OILY AND BLEMISH-PRONE SKIN TYPES

Those with oily or blemish-prone skin may initially feel hesitant to incorporate an oil into their routine. However, Marula Oil has a relatively low comedogenic potential and is rich in oleic acid, which can help balance sebum production in oily skin that is actually dehydrated. For these skin types, focus heavily on the water-based Hydrolyzed Hyaluronic Acid and Decarboxy Carnosine HCl serum during the day to provide weightless hydration. At night, limit the use of Marula Oil to 1 or 2 drops, pressed lightly onto the skin, or mix it directly into a gel-based moisturizer to avoid overloading the skin while still reaping the barrier-repairing benefits.

SENSITIVE SKIN TYPES

One of the greatest advantages of this triad is its high tolerability. Unlike aggressive exfoliating acids or retinoids, which can cause redness and barrier disruption, Marula Oil, Decarboxy Carnosine HCl, and Hydrolyzed Hyaluronic Acid are inherently soothing and supportive of skin barrier resilience. Sensitive skin types can introduce this routine immediately without a transitional buffering period, though patch testing new formulations is always recommended.
