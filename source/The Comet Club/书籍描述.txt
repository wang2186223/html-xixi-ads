Ava spends her nights stargazing on the garage roof with her late mother's old telescope, but she has never told anyone at school. When a new teacher starts an astronomy club, Ava joins three other curious students, and together they take part in a real science program searching for unknown objects in space. Through patience, teamwork, and friendship, the Comet Club makes a discovery that changes everything. An inspiring story about science, perseverance, and the stars that connect us to the people we love.
