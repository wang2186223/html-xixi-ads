For twelve-year-old Nora, the 112-year-old Willowbrook Library is the best place in the world. So when the town council decides to close it, she is determined to find a way to save it. Hidden in a dusty cabinet, she discovers the diary of the library's founder and a mysterious letter that leads to a secret behind the old fireplace. A charming mystery about books, history, and how one young reader can bring a whole community together.
