Jordan is terrified of heights, but his eighth-grade class trip is a six-mile hike to the top of Eagle Peak. His team includes a fast runner who always wants to be first, a quiet bird lover, and a girl recovering from a broken ankle. When they reach the narrow mountain ridge, Jordan must face his biggest fear, and he learns that the hardest climbs are easier when you have friends beside you. An encouraging story about courage, empathy, and teamwork.
