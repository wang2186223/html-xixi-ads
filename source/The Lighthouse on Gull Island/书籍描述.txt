Fourteen-year-old Maya is sent to spend the summer with her grandfather on a tiny island with no internet and nothing to do. But when she helps him restore the old lighthouse for the island's 200th birthday, she discovers the meaning of a promise. And when a storm cuts the power and a fishing boat is lost at sea, Maya must climb 112 steps and keep the light shining with her own two hands. A heartwarming story about family, courage, and finding where you belong.
