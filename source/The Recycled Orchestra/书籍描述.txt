The music room at Lincoln Middle School has been closed for two years, and twelve-year-old Rosa can't afford a real instrument. But when she discovers that buckets, pot lids, and bottles can make music, she has a bold idea: build an entire orchestra out of recycled things. With the help of her friends, the school janitor, and her guitar-playing grandfather, Rosa leads a group of students who turn junk into beautiful music. A creative and inspiring story about imagination, community, and finding your voice.
