On his twelfth birthday, Ethan finally adopts a dog from the shelter: Biscuit, a shy brown dog with one floppy ear whom nobody else wanted. But every afternoon at exactly three-thirty, Biscuit sits by the window and waits. When Ethan discovers who Biscuit is waiting for, he sets out to reunite the loyal dog with his elderly former owner. A touching story about responsibility, kindness, and how love can grow bigger when we share it.
