When Kenji moves from Japan to Maple Street, he feels lonely and worried about his English. Lily remembers what it was like to be the new kid, so she decides to become his friend. When she discovers the beautiful bird-shaped kite his grandmother made for him, Lily comes up with a big idea: a kite festival for the whole neighborhood. A warm and colorful story about kindness, friendship across cultures, and the joy of making someone feel at home.
