During his summer vacation at Coral Bay, eleven-year-old Noah discovers mysterious tracks in the sand that lead to a sea turtle nest. He becomes the youngest volunteer with a marine biologist protecting the eggs, but bright lights from a new beach house threaten to lead the baby turtles in the wrong direction. With a heartfelt letter and a new friend, Noah sets out to help the hatchlings find their way to the ocean. A gentle and hopeful story about nature, conservation, and how one young person can make a difference.
